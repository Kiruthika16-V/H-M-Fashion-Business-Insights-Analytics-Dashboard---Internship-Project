# H-M-Fashion-Business-Insights-Analytics-Dashboard---Internship-Project

# H&M Fashion Business Analytics
A Power BI-based retail analytics project that analyzes category performance, sales seasonality, customer segmentation (RFM), product combinations, and customer retention to identify business growth opportunities for H&M.

# Tools & Techniques
1.Power BI — dashboard design & data visualization
2.Excel — data cleaning & preprocessing
3.DAX — calculated measures & KPIs
4.Data Analytics — trend and performance analysis
5.RFM Analysis — customer segmentation (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)
6.Market Basket Analysis — cross-7.category purchase patterns

# Data Source
This project uses the H&M Personalized Fashion Recommendations dataset from Kaggle.
The raw dataset is not included in this repository due to its size and licensing terms. To reproduce this analysis:
Download the dataset from the link above.
Place the files in a local data/ folder.
Open the .pbix file in Power BI and refresh the data source to point to your local files.
A small cleaned sample is included in data/sample/ for reference on the data schema and structure.

# Key Insights
1.79% of revenue comes from just 3 categories — Upper Body, Lower Body, and Full Body are the major revenue-driving categories.
2.June and September show strong seasonal sales spikes.
3.32% of customers fall into the At Risk segment — the largest single group in the RFM breakdown.
4.81% of revenue comes from repeat customers, underscoring the value of retention over acquisition.
5.Only 14% of baskets span multiple categories, with Upper + Lower Body being the strongest cross-category combination.

# Recommendations
1.Protect the At-Risk customer segment — launch targeted win-back campaigns before these customers churn completely.
2.Invest in the top 3 categories — double down on Upper Body, Lower Body, and Full Body given their outsized revenue contribution.
3.Promote complete-outfit bundles — capitalize on the Upper + Lower Body pairing to increase basket size and multi-category purchases.
4.Plan inventory around seasonal demand — align stock levels and marketing pushes with the June and September peaks.

# Summary
This project turns raw H&M transaction data into actionable business intelligence — identifying where revenue concentrates, which customers are at risk of churn, and how product bundling and seasonality can be leveraged to drive growth.
